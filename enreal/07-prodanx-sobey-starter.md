# ProDanX for Sobey: Kamila's starter pack

**Draft 0.2 · 16 October 2026 · for Kamila.** *Updated after the [team meeting](../../records/meetings/2026-10-16-team-vision/SoftOcean.meeting-notes.md): after her return from Poland, Kamila arranges a meeting with Dan and Tomasz comes with her (D-34); the offer is nothing upfront but not a free try-out (D-35).* Everything Kamila needs for the Sobey Fitness conversation: **what she is offering Dan**, **how one engine makes many apps**, what to show, what to say, and what not to promise. ## 1. What you are offering Dan, in one sentence

> **"Your own Sobey app: one place for your classes, your members and your news, instead of scattered WhatsApp groups and messages."**

## 2. What Dan gets (the value)

| What Dan gets | Why it matters to him | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **His own app with Sobey's name, logo and colours** | It looks and feels like his business, not someone else's | ✅ |
| **One timetable for everything**: Urban Gym, Brute Camp, Trojan Academy, kids' kickboxing, paddleboarding | Members find and book any session in one place | ✅ |
| **Parents can book for their children** | Made for kids' kickboxing | ✅ |
| **Attendance on the night** | He sees who came, and who has not been for a while | ✅ (the list); 🔜 (an automatic nudge when someone stops coming) |
| **A news feed and announcements** | Important news is not lost in WhatsApp chatter | ✅ |
| **A separate chat for each event or meetup** | Questions about one event stay out of the main feed | 💡 |
| **An AI helper that answers common questions** | Saves him texting the same answers | 💡 |
| **Founding-partner terms**: nothing to pay upfront; the first three months free for one group; from month four, the monthly price agreed **in writing before we build**. Not a free try-out | Low risk to start, and a clear deal | Offer |

**The strongest value for a fitness business is keeping members.** People who stop turning up are the ones who leave. The app shows attendance, so Dan can notice and reach out before they go.

## 3. One engine, different apps

**Think of a car factory.** It builds one engine, and puts it into different cars. Each car has its own body, colour and badge. Drivers only see their car.

**ProDanX is the engine.** EnReal and Sobey are two different cars:

| | EnReal app | Sobey app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name and logo** | EnReal / PyPlym | Sobey Fitness |
| **Colours and look** | EnReal's | Sobey's |
| **What's in the timetable** | PyPlym evenings, AI-Bench, AI Workbench | Urban Gym, Brute Camp, Trojan Academy, kids' kickboxing, paddleboarding |
| **Who uses it** | Our community | Dan's members |
| **The engine underneath** | **The same ProDanX**: events, booking, attendance, feed, messages | **The same ProDanX** |

**This already exists, not just on paper.** ProDanX already runs two different-looking apps from the same engine: a green one made for a Ju-Jitsu club, and a blue one Cecy designed as a test. Same engine, different car.

**Why this matters to us:** when we improve the engine for EnReal (for example, easier sign-up), **Sobey gets the improvement too, for free**. We do the work once and offer it many times (document 08).

## 5. Opening the meeting: what to say

*Kamila prefers a set meeting after her return from Poland, with Tomasz there (D-34). She opens with these words; Tomasz then shows the details.*

> "Remember the app idea? Cecy made a mock-up with your logo. Have a look. This is your timetable: Urban Gym, Brute Camp, the kids' classes. Members book here; parents can book for their kids. Your news goes here instead of getting lost in WhatsApp. You can see who's been coming and who hasn't. What do you think?"

Then **listen**. At the end, Tomasz asks plainly whether Dan would like it for one group, on the terms in section 2. A yes on written terms counts; "it looks nice" does not.

## 6. Easy questions to ask over time

1. Roughly how many members are active, and which group is busiest?
2. What takes up most of your week: WhatsApp, bookings, chasing payments?
3. Does Lisa decide these things with you?
4. What do you pay the website company for, roughly?
5. Is there anything you'd never want to lose, like a particular WhatsApp group?

| **"I don't have time."** | "That's why we'd set it up for you and show you and your coaches how it works. Your part is telling members and taking the bookings in it; we'd help with the first weeks." |

## 8. What not to promise

- **Not a replacement for his website.** It works alongside it.
- **Not payments or direct debits.** He keeps his current way of collecting money.
- **Not the AI helper or journals** yet.
- **Not every activity at once.** Start with one group.
- **Not "it's ready".** Say "we'd set it up for you", and Tomasz confirms what is ready.
