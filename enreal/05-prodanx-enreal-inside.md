## 1. Why we are doing this

**Tomasz wants to stop selling only his hours.** The first target is **£3,000 a month** through SoftOcean, enough for him to work one day a week less on his contract.

EnReal is how we try to get there, **while learning as cheaply as possible**. Until mid-December we are learning, not yet earning. That is deliberate.

## 3. What an issue is

Each month, **one topic**, for example "learning better with AI without letting it think for you". The issue contains:
1. **A research note** by **Cecy**: what studies say about how people learn, decide or work with AI.
2. **A lab note** by **Tomasz**: what he built or tested on the topic.
3. **A 30-minute "try this"** that anyone can do.
4. **A live online session** with questions, recorded.
5. **An optional challenge:** build something small, alone or in a group of 3–4 (a "pod"). Nobody has to build. Those who do get recognised with badges and in the showcase.

**The same topic comes to PyPlym in Plymouth the following month.** We prepare once and use it twice.

| | Anyone | Free member | Paid member |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The first part of each note | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Try-this, challenge, pods | — | Yes | Yes |
| **Full notes, live session, recordings, library** | — | — | **Yes** |

**Issue #1 is completely free,** so people can judge it. In mid-December we ask whether they want to pay (about £8–12 a month, still to decide) for Issue #2.
