# ProDanX and EnReal: showcase (website and marketing deck)

**Draft 0.1 · 16 October 2026.** The vision behind (1) a new **case study on softocean.net**, alongside PlanktoSpace, and (2) a short marketing deck built from it. Audience: website visitors, prospective software and consulting clients, clubs and communities that might want their own app, and people who might join EnReal. Labels: **[P]** planned; **[F]** founder.

**Positioning rules (decided 16 October):**

- SoftOcean's identity stays **high-technology software and hands-on consulting, without forgetting the human**. This page is **one case study in that portfolio**, not a new identity.
- **PlanktoSpace remains the flagship** until this work has comparable evidence.
- Positioning **now**: *"We have built a configurable community platform, and are preparing it for our own community's launch in January."* **From January**, once it is live: *"We build software for communities, and run our own community on it."* **Later**, once Sobey and EnReal have results: a proven community builder.
- Nothing is described as working before it does. The page is **updated as evidence arrives**.

---

## 1. The case study in one sentence

> **SoftOcean built ProDanX, a platform that gives each community its own branded app, and is preparing it to run its own AI community, EnReal, from January 2027, so you will be able to see it working before you ask for it.** *(Switch to "runs" only after the first event.)*

## 2. The page, section by section

| # | Section | Content | Status label shown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The challenge** | Clubs, studios and communities run on scattered tools: WhatsApp groups, booking websites, spreadsheets, social media. Messages get lost; admin piles up; members drift away unnoticed. | — |
| 2 | **What we built** | **ProDanX**: one platform that gives each organisation **its own app** (its name, colours and layout) for events and bookings, attendance, a community feed, announcements and messages, with parents able to book for children. Each organisation's data is kept separate. | Built |
| 3 | **How we built it** | Planned delivery: requirements first, documented decisions, tests and an independent security and architecture review. A July 2026 review of the source code (not of a live system) found **no critical issues** and confirmed real separation of each community's data; it also listed four high-priority fixes to make before real members join. Say all three parts or none. A modern, AI-assisted engineering workflow with human design and review at every step. | Built; source reviewed July 2026; pre-launch fixes open |
| 4 | **Where it runs** | **EnReal**, SoftOcean's own community for practical AI in Plymouth, is planned to run on ProDanX: **online from 20 November 2026** if the platform route is ready (otherwise on ready-made tools first), and **PyPlym evenings from January 2027**. Events, sign-ups, attendance and materials. | **Planned**; then live, with numbers |
| 5 | **The human side** | Software designed around the people who use it: **to be** tested by a non-technical operator before any customer sees it (planned; first evidence by PyPlym event 2); built from what members and organisers actually do. The same "human first" thinking behind PlanktoSpace. | — |
| 6 | **What it could do for you** | Your own branded app for your club, studio or community, without building from scratch. | — |

| 8 | **Work with SoftOcean** | Hands-on consulting and software: requirements, planned delivery, AI and data systems, community apps. | — |
